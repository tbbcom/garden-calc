Quick answer

To find how much mulch you need, multiply bed length × width (feet) × depth (inches), then divide by 324. The result is cubic yards. One cubic yard covers 108 sq ft at 3 inches deep, the depth most beds need. That equals 13.5 bags of 2 cu ft or 9 bags of 3 cu ft.

How the mulch calculator works

The calculator adds up the area of every bed you enter (rectangles, circles, triangles and rings around trees), multiplies it by the depth of mulch you still need to add, and converts the volume into cubic yards, bags and weight. It subtracts any mulch already on the bed, adds an allowance for settling, and compares the cost of bags with a bulk delivery so you can see which is cheaper.

The mulch formula

  • Cubic feet = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12
  • Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27, or square feet × depth (in) ÷ 324
  • Bags = cubic feet ÷ bag size (cu ft), rounded up
  • Circle area = 3.1416 × (diameter ÷ 2)²; triangle = base × height ÷ 2

Worked example: a 10 × 20 ft bed (200 sq ft) at 3 in deep needs 200 × 3 ÷ 324 = 1.85 cubic yards, which is 50 cubic feet, or 25 bags of 2 cu ft. With 10% extra for settling (55 cu ft, 2.04 cubic yards), buy 28 bags, or order 2–2.5 cubic yards in bulk.

Avoid "volcano mulching": never pile mulch against tree trunks or plant stems. Keep a 3–6 in bare ring around the trunk. Deep mulch against bark traps moisture and invites rot, rodents and disease.

Bagged vs bulk mulch: which is cheaper?

Bags win for small jobs because there's no delivery fee and they're easy to carry. Bulk usually wins somewhere between 1.5 and 3 cubic yards (roughly 20–40 bags), depending on your local delivery charge. Enter your own prices in step 3 and the calculator shows the break-even for your project.

Mulch calculator FAQ

How much mulch do I need for a 10 × 10 area?

A 10 × 10 ft area (100 sq ft) needs 0.93 cubic yards at 3 inches deep, which is 25 cubic feet or 13 bags of 2 cu ft. At 2 inches you need 0.62 cubic yards (9 bags).

How many bags of mulch are in a yard?

One cubic yard equals 27 cubic feet: 13.5 bags of 2 cu ft, 9 bags of 3 cu ft, or 18 bags of 1.5 cu ft.

How many square feet does a bag of mulch cover?

A 2 cu ft bag covers 12 sq ft at 2 inches deep or 8 sq ft at 3 inches. A 3 cu ft bag covers 18 sq ft at 2 inches or 12 sq ft at 3 inches.

Should I remove old mulch before adding new mulch?

Usually not. Rake the old layer to break up any crust, then top it up so the total stays within 2–4 inches. Enter the existing depth in the calculator and it only counts what you need to add. Remove old mulch only if it's moldy, matted or already deeper than 4 inches.

How much does a cubic yard of mulch weigh?

Wood mulch weighs roughly 400–800 lb per cubic yard depending on type and moisture. Compost is about 1,000–1,600 lb, and pea gravel or river rock about 2,700–2,900 lb (around 1.4 tons). Check your truck's payload before hauling bulk material.

How much mulch do I need around a tree?

For a 6 ft wide ring at 3 inches deep with a 1 ft bare circle at the trunk, you need about 7 cubic feet (4 bags of 2 cu ft). Choose "Ring around a tree" in the calculator and enter how many trees you have.

How often should I add mulch?

Most organic mulches break down within 1–2 years. Top up about 1 inch each spring, or 2 inches for fine mulch that decomposes quickly, to keep the total at 2–3 inches.
